About Treevit

Treevit is an open-source initiative designed to democratize access to advanced AI assistance. Built with privacy and performance in mind, it bridges the gap between complex model inference and user-friendly experience.

Whether you are a developer debugging code, a writer crafting stories, or a student learning new concepts, Treevit is engineered to be your ultimate intelligent companion.
