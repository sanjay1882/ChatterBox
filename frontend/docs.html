Chatterbox Docs

Everything you need to know about using Chatterbox AI assistant

What Can It Do?

  • Write Anything

    Essays, stories, emails, blogs - you name it. Just tell me what you need.

  • Translate Languages

    Convert text between English, Hindi, Tamil, and other languages.

  • Analyze Content

    Get reviews, summaries, and breakdowns of movies, books, and more.

  • Voice Commands

    Use your microphone to speak instead of typing.

  • Save & Share

    Copy, download, or share your conversations easily.

Pro Tip: Hover over my responses to see action buttons for copying, sharing, and more.

How to Get the Best Results

Getting Started

  • Type your question in the chat box at the bottom
  • Click the suggestions for quick ideas
  • Press Enter or click the send button
  • Use the microphone icon for voice input

Writing Better Prompts

Good: "Write a story about a robot learning emotions"
Better: "Write a creative story about a robot learning human emotions, make it heartwarming"

Good: "Explain climate change"
Better: "Explain climate change in simple terms for a 10-year-old"

Good: "Translate this"
Better: "Translate this paragraph to Tamil, keep it formal"
Remember: The more specific you are, the better I can help you!

Try These Examples

For Writing

"Create a blog post about healthy eating habits for students"

For Translation

"Translate this recipe from English to Hindi"
